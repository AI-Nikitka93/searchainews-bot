## Verdict

SearchAInews remains a readable utilitarian Telegram bot, but the visual layer is still `USABLE / NEEDS POLISH`, not mature. The best surface is onboarding: it has one task, one short question, and simple choices. The weakest surfaces are personal digest and main menu: they ask the user to parse too much at once and do not create a strong primary/secondary hierarchy.

Compared with the 2026-04-28 visual audit, the core findings remain open. The current code also allows a personal digest to include up to 4 action items, which increases the mobile reading load.

## Top Problems

### VIS-001 High - Personal digest is too dense for a mobile Telegram card
- **Status:** open
- **Evidence:** OBSERVED in `2026-06-01_visual_preview_mobile_390.png`; SUPPORTED_BY_CODE in `cf_worker/src/utils/text.ts:49-70`.
- **Finding:** a single news card can include long title, score, rationale up to 360 chars, heading, up to 4 action bullets, and source. On a narrow phone this becomes the dominant screen and pushes the next action below the visible area.
- **Impact:** the main value proposition is useful, but the visual form makes quick scanning harder than it should be.

### VIS-002 High - Main menu keeps too many equal-weight actions
- **Status:** open
- **Evidence:** OBSERVED in `2026-06-01_visual_preview_laptop_1366.png`; SUPPORTED_BY_CODE in `cf_worker/src/keyboards/menu.ts:5-17`.
- **Finding:** `10 новостей`, `20 новостей`, settings, role, language, subscription, about, help are all presented as similar buttons. Only the first button is slightly emphasized in the preview; Telegram itself may flatten this even further.
- **Impact:** the primary action is present, but users must still compare too many controls before acting.

### VIS-003 High - Personal digest and channel post use different visual languages
- **Status:** open
- **Evidence:** OBSERVED side-by-side in `2026-06-01_visual_preview_laptop_1366.png`; SUPPORTED_BY_CODE in `cf_worker/src/utils/text.ts:49-70` and `cf_worker/src/services/channel.ts:939-1017`.
- **Finding:** channel posts are more compact and editorial: title, short summary, one `Что проверить`, source. Personal digest remains a technical card with score, rationale and a list.
- **Impact:** the product feels less consistent than it is functionally; the channel teaches a clearer pattern than the bot reuses.

### VIS-004 Medium - Empty/no-role/subscribed states compete for attention
- **Status:** open
- **Evidence:** OBSERVED in `2026-06-01_visual_preview_mobile_390.png`; SUPPORTED_BY_CODE in `cf_worker/src/bot.ts:53-71`, `cf_worker/src/utils/i18n.ts:39-55`.
- **Finding:** no-role, no-news and subscription state can appear as separate high-prominence messages. In the preview they read like three independent cards instead of one clear next step.
- **Impact:** not blocking, but it weakens recovery and increases perceived complexity during failure or first-run states.

### VIS-005 Medium - Brand and visual QA layer are audit-only, not product-owned
- **Status:** open
- **Evidence:** OBSERVED by asset scan; audit screenshots exist under `docs/audit/evidence`, but no owned product asset kit was found.
- **Finding:** the visual identity is mostly name + Telegram defaults + emoji. There is no stable avatar mark, visual regression pack, or message design contract in product docs.
- **Impact:** acceptable for a utility bot, but it limits recognizability and makes future template changes harder to review consistently.

## Strengths
- Onboarding is visually simple and understandable.
- Role selection is compact and does not overload the first task.
- Channel post structure is the strongest current pattern and should become the reference for bot cards.
- No obvious dark-pattern issue was observed; the interface does not hide refusal or create fake urgency.

## Highest-Impact Fixes

1. Make personal digest match the channel pattern: title, 1-2 sentence summary, one `Что проверить`, source. Keep score/rationale detail behind a secondary command or only show it for high-impact items.
2. Reduce action bullets in bot cards from 4 to 1-2 visible actions; reserve longer action lists for `/details` or a follow-up message.
3. Split the menu into primary actions and secondary settings. Primary row: `10 новостей`, `20 новостей`, subscription. Secondary row: role, language, about/help.
4. Merge empty/no-role recovery into one dominant next step. If role is missing, show role picker only; do not stack unrelated status messages at the same prominence.
5. Add a small message design contract in docs: max title length, summary length, action count, section order, emoji policy, and 4-5 canonical visual states.
